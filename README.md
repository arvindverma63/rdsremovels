# RDS Removals & Storage - Modern Multi-Page Website

A clean, modern, multi-page website mockup for **RDS Removals & Storage** (Shaftesbury, Wiltshire, Dorset & Nationwide UK).

## 📄 Dedicated Multi-Page Architecture

1. **[Home (`index.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/index.html)**:
   - High-impact photographic full-background hero showcase with fast postcode lookup quote card.
   - Key company metrics strip (£50k insurance, 15+ years, 4.9★ rating).
   - Core services summary preview with checklists.
   - 4-step moving timeline overview.
   - Direct call-to-action banner for free survey quotations.

2. **[Services (`services.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/services.html)**:
   - Detailed breakdown of all 6 core services: Residential Home Removals, Secure Storage Vaults, Office & Commercial Relocations, Man with a Van, Packing Materials & Crates, and Nationwide UK Direct Routes.
   - Comprehensive feature checklists and 1-click survey booking.

3. **[Why Choose Us (`about.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/about.html)**:
   - Company standards: DBS-checked permanent staff, £50,000 goods in transit protection, floor & furniture armor, and fixed-price guarantees.
   - Comprehensive 4-stage moving journey.

4. **[Areas Covered (`locations.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/locations.html)**:
   - Dedicated regional town guides for Shaftesbury (Depot Hub), Salisbury, Warminster, Gillingham, Frome, Yeovil, Wincanton, Bath, London & Nationwide UK.
   - Proximity mileage and transit times.

5. **[Reviews (`reviews.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/reviews.html)**:
   - 4.9 / 5.0 verified customer score overview with 98.6% recommendation rate.
   - Verified feedback cards from residential homeowners, commercial relocations, and storage clients.

6. **[Contact Us (`contact.html`)](file:///d:/laravel/rdsremoves%20mockup/contact.html)**:
   - Direct phone hotline (`01747 859213`), email (`mail@rdsremovals.co.uk`), depot facility location, operating hours, and online survey request form.

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic Standards
- **Typography**: Google Font `Roboto` (`300`, `400`, `500`, `700`, `900`) for all headings and body copy in classic WordPress style.
- **Clean Light Theme**: Crisp pure white cards, soft neutral background (`#f8fafc`), deep navy headings (`#0f172a`), and royal blue primary accents (`#2563eb`).
- **Professional SVG Icons**: Monochromatic vector line icons styled directly with theme CSS variables — zero odd emojis.
- **WordPress Elevation**: Smooth CSS hover elevations (`transform: translateY(-3px)`) and soft drop shadows.
- **Universal Quote Modal**: Accessible across every page with service pre-filling and instant confirmation dialogs.
